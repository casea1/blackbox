## What's new

### One job per report page (PAGES1)

The same per-system health squares were on the Overview, the Systems page and Audit health. Each page now has one job:

- **Overview:** the summary cards, Detections and Needs attention. The "Systems at a glance" table is gone. Needs attention lists what to look at and links to it.
- **Systems:** a list of the computers, with one **Status** column saying what needs a look ("An event log cleared · 1 open CAT I finding", "not reporting") or **OK**. The six check columns are gone, and the name opens the system's own page.
- **Audit health:** unchanged. It's the one place with the check-by-system grid, the settings to fix, and the fix list.

## What's fixed

- **Setup:** "Not on a network (standalone)" now shows directly under **This computer only**, and only while that choice is selected.
