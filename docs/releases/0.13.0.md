## What's new

From the ISSOs' review of the reports.

### Reports

- **Antivirus definitions date.** Audit health has an Antivirus table: each system's antivirus (Microsoft Defender or ClamAV), the date its definitions were made, their age, the version, real-time protection or the ClamAV service, and the result. The Overview checklist has an "Antivirus definitions" line with the oldest date, and each system's health list shows its date; both link to the table. Checks now record the date itself, not only in the text.
- **All reports.** The date range at the top of a report no longer opens the list of reports. An **All reports** button, and an entry in the menu, does. The list is titled "All reports", explains itself, marks the newest report **Latest**, has a button to open it, and opens a report when its row is clicked.
- **People: clickable activity.** In "When they were active", a red hour shows only that person's detections in that hour. Any other hour opens Search with their events in that hour of the week.
- **Clickable boxes.** The boxes at the top of each event page filter the table below them (lockouts, new USB devices, files copied, accounts created or disabled, logs cleared, audit policy changes, suspicious PowerShell, malware, new services, remote logons). The first box shows everything again. Audit health's boxes go to the matrix or the gaps.

### Trends

- **What changed, on the Overview.** The biggest moves this week against the average of the earlier reports, in plain sentences: network-wide counts up or down by 25% or more, systems with more detections than usual, and people with more privileged actions than usual or with privileged actions for the first time. Each line links to the page behind it.
- **A person's activity over time.** Each report now keeps counts by person in `summary.json` (`people`: privileged actions, logons, failed logons, after-hours actions, detections). Each person's page has an "Over time" panel with this week against their own average and a chart of their privileged actions by week. The Trends page adds "Privileged actions by person, by week". Reports made before 0.13 have no counts by person, so their weeks are blank.
